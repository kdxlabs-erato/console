# ADR-0152: An agent does everything it can itself, and asks for help only with what it cannot do

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-24
- Adds to [ADR-0088](0088-an-agent-account-has-a-desk-and-is-set-up-from-an-invitation-by-signing-in-as-itself.md), points 6 and 7, under which what an agent cannot do is a decision to whoever can do it. Nothing of it is taken back.

## Context

An agent account met steps of its first task that it could not do, and asked for them as decisions. Its manager asked why it had not done its own task itself.

- **The rule stood in the desk, and the task did not say it.** A desk says "I do everything I can myself", and names a judgment and what cannot be undone as what is asked. The first task said how to ask only above the list of what a workspace's services may leave an account without: a task whose services ask for nothing said nothing of asking.
- **The first answer named the steps.** A proposal made those steps a person's from the start, each with what the person does and where. It took from every agent what many can do, wrote what one product would not do on one day into every account's first task, and gave a person work before any agent had found that it could not do it.

## Decision

1. **One rule: an agent does everything it can itself, and asks for help only with what it cannot do.** The first task and the skill say it in those words, and the desk in its own.
2. **What it cannot do is the agent's own to judge.** No word of the console's names a kind of work as a person's from the start, or lists what an agent cannot do: that differs by product and by day, and the agent is the one that meets it.
3. **Help is asked as everything else is:** a decision, of whoever can do it and of its manager where it does not know who, with the exact values and what to hand back, and the agent goes on with the rest. The first task says so whatever the workspace's services are, and what a service may leave the account without follows where there is any.

## Consequences

- **The same words serve an agent that can do a step and one that cannot,** and a person is asked for less as products do more, with nothing here to change.
- **What an agent will ask for is not known beforehand.** A manager reads it in the decision, as it comes.
- **A desk written before keeps its `AGENTS.md`:** its account adds the words itself. A first task made before keeps its words.
- **Rejected:** steps named as a person's from the start, for the reasons above; and a list of what an agent cannot do, each with what to ask, which reads as a list of what it need not try.
