# ADR-0123: Charts are parts, placed by measures and drawn by the stylesheet

- Status: Accepted; the order of the six colours of point 4 and two of their values amended by [ADR-0134](0134-the-coral-is-the-second-colour-of-a-series-and-a-calmer-one.md)
- Date: 2026-11-15
- Builds on [ADR-0106](0106-a-look-is-a-part-with-a-file-and-a-row-and-a-page-places-its-parts.md) (a look is a part), [ADR-0054](0054-a-plugins-pages-run-in-a-sandboxed-frame-and-speak-only-to-their-plugin.md) (a plugin's frame and its policy) and [ADR-0040](0040-the-pages-are-light-by-default.md) (one look, light).

## Context

A desk is grown by what its member asks ([ADR-0122](0122-a-desk-is-given-its-least-and-grown-by-what-its-member-asks.md)), and what a member asks to see is, as often as not, figures: how something did, by week, against the rest. A plugin's pages show figures too. The console had nothing to draw them with. Its tokens carried six colours for a series, for the plugins' pages, and no part drew in them; a page that wanted a bar drew its own, and a look written on a page is what the parts exist to end.

Three things bound what a chart can be here. A look is written once, as a part, and a component sets a style on an element only for a measure it computed, which the styles' check counts. A plugin's page runs in a frame whose policy lets no style be set on an element at all. And the console has one look, light, which a console of one's own re-skins by its tokens.

## Decision

1. **Eight parts, like any other.** `series` (the colour a series is drawn in), `legend`, `stat` (a figure with its name, its change and its run), `spark` (a sparkline), `bars` (a ranking or a comparison), `share` (one whole in its parts), `chart` (columns or lines over time) and `figures` (a table of figures). Each is a file under `styles/parts/`, a row of the catalogue, a place on `/parts` with its edges (nothing, one point, a long name, amounts of very different size, a narrow box) and a component: `Legend`, `Stat` and `Stats`, `Sparkline`, `BarList`, `ShareBar`, `Chart`, `FigureTable`.
2. **A chart is placed by measures and drawn by the stylesheet.** How long a bar is, how high a column rises and where a line runs are attributes of an SVG, each a share of a hundred of the box the part's class gives it (`width="62%"`). Nothing is a pixel computed from a width a script measured, and nothing is a style: the colours, the strokes, the radii and the box itself are the part's, in its file. So a chart is as wide as where it is put without a script watching its size, what the server could render is what the browser shows, the frame's policy holds, and the check's count of styles is as it was.
3. **The measures are a module with no dependency.** `charts.ts`, which is `@skillcdn/console/charts`: the scale and its round marks, where each amount stands, a column, a bar's length, the parts of a whole, a sparkline's points, which points are labelled. The components lean on React and on it alone. A plugin's plain bundle draws the parts' classes with the measures, and a bundle of React takes the components, without either taking the console.
4. **Six colours for a series, held to checks and not to taste.** The hues are the console's own, those of its categories, and a coral between its pink and its orange, each stepped in lightness with the hue held, until: any two of the six, not neighbours alone, are 8 or more apart under protanopia and deuteranopia and 15 or more apart to full colour vision (distance in OKLab, times a hundred), since lines cross and any part may stand beside any other; and each is 3:1 or better against the console's light grounds and against dark ones, so that a console re-skinned dark keeps them. Blue, teal, raspberry, coral, violet, green. No orange, which is the point's own colour, and no yellow, which at the lightness that stands out on both grounds is a brown: with the other five as they are, those two families are the only ones in which a sixth passes every pair, so the green was stepped a little darker, and a coral passes there. What is past the sixth, or the rest folded into one, wears the neutral, never a seventh hue. A series' colour is for marks; a name stands beside its key in the colour of text. The tones stay what they are: a change is coloured as good or bad only where the page says which it is, and its sign says the direction either way.
5. **A chart has what a reader needs and no more.** One scale to a chart, from zero, in round steps: two measures of different size are two charts. A legend for two series or more, none for one, which the heading names. Columns thin however much room a point has; a bar and a column square where they start and round where they end; parts of a whole apart by a gap in the surface's colour, never by a line round them. The points' names as many as fit, fewer in a narrow box, the last always. An amount beside one a thousand times its size is still drawn as something.
6. **A chart says what it shows to a reader who cannot see it.** The figures of a list of bars, of a share and of a figure stand beside the drawing as words; a chart over time has its figures under it as a table for a screen reader, and the drawing is hidden from one. A point says its figures to the pointer over it through the browser's own tooltip.

## Consequences

- The six colours changed their values, and slots 2, 3, 4 and 6 their hue: they were orange, green, ochre and teal, the first two the point's and the success tone's own colours, and as a set they failed the checks above (the teal too grey, the ochre and the green 5.5 apart under protanopia). Nothing in this repository drew with them; a plugin that did is drawn in the new ones.
- No dark theme comes with this. The colours are checked on dark grounds so that they hold where a console sets one by its tokens.
- A plugin's plain bundle writes the parts' markup itself, with the measures. A second renderer, for the DOM, would have to be held equal to the components; it is written when a plugin asks for it.
- The pointer's answer is a tooltip of the browser's, late and plain. One placed by a script would be a place set as a style, which a frame refuses.
- The ready page of a desk draws its figures with these parts ([ADR-0116](0116-a-desks-dashboard-is-a-page-of-a-console-of-ones-own-read-as-it-is-looked-at.md), [ADR-0117](0117-a-desk-keeps-records-of-what-its-member-made-as-files-the-desks-pages-read.md)): what it shows is as it was.
- The coral is 13 from the point and 16 from the danger tone to full colour vision, lighter than both: apart, and not far. A series in it is one more reason a page says good and bad in words and tones, never by which series a thing is.
- Rejected: a charting library (a dependency with a look of its own, sized by scripts and styled on elements); measuring the box with a script (state in every chart, nothing drawn until it has run, and no use to a plain bundle); a round chart for a whole in its parts (angles are read worse than lengths); a second scale on one chart; keeping the six colours as they were.
