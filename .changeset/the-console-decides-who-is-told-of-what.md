The console decides who is told of what happens on the board, and keeps the record (ADR-0157). The worker makes a notification for each member an event concerns, a person and an agent account by the same rules: a task given to them, ready to begin, moved, changed, commented on or put away, a decision that waits for them or one of theirs answered, an agent account that reports to them standing otherwise. Each is a kind a member has on or off. Migration `0068` adds `notifications`, `task_follows` and `people.notifications`.

- **A bell in the head of every page.** It is marked while something is not read, and the list opens under it, the newest first: who did what in two lines at most, with why, where and when under them. Opening the list reads all of it, and what was new until then is marked that once. There is no page and no tab of its own for it.
- **What a member is told of is a view of the settings**, `/settings/notifications`. A person who chose nothing is told of their agent accounts alone.
- **A task has a bell, on or off.** On to begin with where the task is theirs and a kind they have on would tell them of it, off for any other. Turned on where it was off, the task tells them of everything on it; turned off, of nothing; turned back, by their kinds as before.
- **A plugin sends on what the console told, and no more.** The capability `notifications:read` hands a hook, with each console event, whom the console told of it: `data.told`, each `{ personId, kind, woken }`, `woken` where the console woke the member's agent account itself. A kind a member turned off is sent by nobody; of the kinds they have on, a member chooses in their settings which a plugin sends too, and `told` leaves out whoever turned a kind off for that plugin. The event itself reaches the plugin as before, whoever was told. Such a plugin hears an event once the board's pass has read it, a few seconds after it happened. A manifest that names the capability needs `console: ">=0.6.1"`.
- **An agent account is woken for what it was woken for**, where it chose nothing: a notification is what its wake is sent as, made with it or not at all, so that a pass that ends halfway wakes it once, and read as it is made. It may turn a kind off, turn a task off, or have a kind wake it that did not, a task of its own moved, changed or put away.
- **From the command:** `console notifications`, which lists and marks as read; `console notify`, with `--via <plugin>` for what a plugin sends; `console follow <number>` and `console mute <number>`, which turn a task on and off. `console task <number>` says whether a task's notifications are on.
- **REST:** `GET /api/v1/notifications`; `POST /api/v1/notifications/read`, which answers `at`, the moment it marked them read at; `GET` and `PUT /api/v1/notifications/settings`, with `carriers`, the plugins that send notifications on and the kinds turned off for each; `GET` and `PUT .../tasks/<id>/follow`, `{ on, usual, all }` and `{ on }`; `/api/v1/me` answers `notifications.unread`.
- **For a console of one's own:** `@skillcdn/console/api` exports the kinds, the defaults, `notificationKindFor`, `carriedBy`, `taskToldByDefault`, `taskTold`, `taskFollowToKeep` and `TASK_KINDS_OF`, and the client the calls. The package exports the components `NotificationsMenu`, `NotificationSettings` and `TaskBell`; `Shell` takes `notifications`, what stands in its head before the person's menu, and `TaskView` takes `follow`. The `menu` part has a menu opened from a glyph and a menu that is a list (`sc-menu-summary-glyph`, `sc-menu-panel-list`, `sc-menu-head`, `sc-menu-rows`, `sc-menu-row`, `sc-menu-note`, `sc-menu-foot`), the `count` part a dot for something new (`sc-nav-dot`), the `button` part a button that is a glyph alone (`sc-button-glyph`), and the icon pack `bell-off`. In the language packs, `notifications`, `settings.views.notifications` and `plugins.capabilities["notifications:read"]` are new.

A console before this version answers none of it: the pages show the bell where `/api/v1/me` counts notifications, and the command says so of a console that has no such address.
