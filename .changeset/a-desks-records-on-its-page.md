A desk's page opens on the desk's records (ADR-0117): what its member made and what became of it, read from the files the desk keeps under `records/`, which `console serve --records` serves at `/records/`. `DeskRecords` composes the views, each exported and built from the console's parts: the collections with their items counted by status (`DeskCollections`), a collection's items as cards with their previews or as rows, narrowed by status (`DeskCollectionView`), an item with its files shown in place by the kind the server says each is, its fields, its links, where it was used and how it did by day (`DeskItemView`), and a collection's results, each metric in all and by week, the ratios, and the items that did best by a metric (`DeskCollectionResults`). What reads them is exported too: `readDeskRecords`, `readCollection` and `probeFile`, over the page's own `fetch` or a `RecordsReader` given to `deskPage({ records })`. A record was written by an agent: every file is bounded and parsed, what does not parse is named in a line and the rest is shown, every word is text, a link is taken only as https, and a file only from under its collection's `files/`.
