A plugin's job that keeps failing is told to a reader who may not read why in words that say so. The problem sent everyone to `plugin.failed` among the plugin's events; that event is written in the plugin's board project, and a member the project is closed to reads none of it. Such a reader is now told that the reason is in that project, by its key, and that a member of it or an administrator reads it. The reason itself is still said nowhere but in the event.
