A part of a skill says what it lacks, and a page written is given with its address.

- A part of a long skill that is not its last now ends, after the line that names the next part's command, with the console's own words: that the part stops in the middle of the skill, that its later steps, checkpoints and rules are in the parts that follow, and that the command is run now, before a plan, an answer or any other step, until a part ends without those lines. A fresh agent read one part of four, saw that the skill continued, and planned from that part.
- The console's rules say the same in a clause: a long skill comes in parts, and every part is read before any is acted on, a plan included.
- `console write` prints the page's address on a line `page: <address>`, as `console task` and `console decision` print theirs, so that an agent tells a person where the page is and hands it in.
