Two more parts and two more lines of text, for looks the console's pages each drew for themselves:

- `sheet` (`sc-sheet`, `-large`, `-raised`, `-hover`): a white card laid on a pane, a row of a list, a thing to choose, a note; a fine edge and a small corner, a larger corner for a card that stands by itself, a shadow under it, an edge that firms under the pointer where it leads somewhere. Its padding is the page's.
- `divider` (`sc-divider`, `sc-divider-below`, `sc-divided`): the hairline over an element, under it, or between each child of a list and the one before.
- `text` has `sc-small`, a line smaller than the page's in its own colour, and `sc-fine-faint`, fine print fainter still.

A settings group's title is `sc-subtitle`, which the console's settings pages give `sc-settings-group-title` beside; a plugin's page that names `sc-settings-group-title` alone keeps the same look.

Pages of the console that wear them, and look as they did: a token, a document's row and its archived notice, a skill and its address, the ways to connect a pack, a category, a decision's row in the record and on a person's work, a plugin's permission, a session, a team's members and key, the front page's glance rows and how many more, a goal's progress, the roadmap's undated line, a comment's files and the files chosen for it (now `sc-chosen-files`, `sc-chosen-file`), a document's versions, an agent's secrets and a run's header. The page classes that drew those looks no longer do.
