`console skill <path>` takes a skill's folder as well as the path of its `SKILL.md`: `console skill engineering/skills/review` reads `engineering/skills/review/SKILL.md`, and `.` the root's own. Where the pack answers that no skill, or no file, is at a path, the command says that, with the path it read and how the pack is listed, in place of the words about a private pack and its token, which are now kept for a pack that serves nothing to the console. After a listing and after what `console skills --search` found, a line says how a skill is loaded whole, its rules first: a skill read with `--file` comes without the rules of its pack.
