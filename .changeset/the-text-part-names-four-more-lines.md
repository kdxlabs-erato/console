The `text` part has four more classes, for lines the pages each drew for themselves: `sc-note`, a line that says more about what it stands by; `sc-fine`, the same a size down; `sc-name`, the name of the thing a row is about; and `sc-subtitle`, the title of a group inside a pane. A plugin's pages may name them like any other part. The console's own pages look as they did.

Eight classes of the console's own pages carried nothing but such a look and are gone, their elements wearing the part's class instead: `sc-agent-proposal-name`, `sc-agent-proposal-handle`, `sc-agent-state-why`, `sc-talk-who`, `sc-token-name`, `sc-pack-way-title`, `sc-plugin-tick-under` and `sc-sign-in-lead`. A page's class was never a part, but a stylesheet of your own that named one of these no longer finds it.
