Breaking: the icon pack is a duotone (ADR-0149), and most of its glyphs look otherwise: under its lines a glyph may have one part tinted in the accent, the one area that tells what the thing is, a calendar's head, a folder's front, a lock's body. `GlyphShape` has an optional third place, `"tint"`, and `Icon` and `iconElement` give that shape the class `sc-icon-tint`, which the `icon` part colours in `--sc-color-accent-solid` at 30%; without the stylesheet it is drawn in the text's colour, faint. The tint goes away at `xs`, on a filled button, on a project's mark, in a place of a tone of its own (a danger button, a callout or a badge of a tone), and where a glyph stands in a line of text and takes `sc-icon-plain`; it deepens on a thing chosen and greys where a thing cannot be used. `pnpm icons` takes a shape filled in the accent with `stroke="none"`, before the lines, as a tinted part, two at most.

A page that draws `GLYPHS` itself reads the third place: a shape marked `"tint"` is filled and never stroked, and a reader that strokes every shape draws an outline round it. A glyph that should stay lines alone anywhere else, in a line of text say, takes `sc-icon-plain`.

The pack has four more glyphs, for an editor's tools: `undo`, `redo`, `list-numbered` and `quote`. The settings gear is redrawn with six teeth; the calendar, document, folder, comment, bell, person, lock, search and home a little rounder; download and upload as an arrow into and out of a tray, the numbered list as two numbers on a tinted gutter, the quote as two marks over the lines they open; and the image, the eye, the clock, the envelope, the book, the external link and the chart with the tint on the part that tells. Every glyph stands inside the margin and in the middle of the grid, and boxes of one size share one corner. Every glyph keeps its name.
