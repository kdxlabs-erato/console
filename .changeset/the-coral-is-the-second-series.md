Breaking: the colours of a series run blue, coral, raspberry, teal, violet, green. The coral is second, where the teal was, and the teal fourth, so that a chart of two series, the commonest, puts the blue beside the coral: the pair that stands furthest apart to full colour vision and to a reader who does not tell red from green. The coral is calmer (`#e45b63`, where it was `#fa4655`), and the raspberry a step deeper (`#bf1b7f`, where it was `#bb2b76`) to stay apart from it; the six pass the same checks as before. A plugin that drew with `--sc-series-2`, `--sc-series-3` or `--sc-series-4` draws its series in the new colours.
