What a sub-agent is called, and the end of what a product wrote on its standard error, are cut after the account's secrets are out of them, as a tool's input is. A sub-agent's name was cut to 80 characters before, and a value that crossed the 80th kept its beginning in the transcript; the end of a product's standard error that a session's last error shows could begin with the end of a value whose beginning was let go. A session's driver is now handed `cutEnd` beside `cut`, which keeps the end of a text the same way, and `errorsShown` shows what a product wrote on its standard error through it, the last 4,000 characters; `createSpawn` keeps the last 16,000 and begins them with `…` where it let the beginning go. `cutShownEnd`, `plainCutEnd`, `errorsShown` and `MAX_ERRORS_SHOWN` are exported from `@skillcdn/console/runner` for a driver of one's own.
