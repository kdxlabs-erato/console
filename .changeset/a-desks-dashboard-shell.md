The package ships the shell of a desk's pages, `template/desk-web`: the four files of a console of one's own that opens on one page of the member's own, empty until they say what to show, built with `npm run build` and served with the desk's records beside it by `console serve dist --records ../records`. Two words in it are filled by the command that writes a desk: `__VERSION__` in `package.json` and `__TITLE__` in `index.html` and the README.
