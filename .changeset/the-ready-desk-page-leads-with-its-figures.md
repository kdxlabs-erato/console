The ready desk page (`deskPage()`) is composed as an example of a page of figures built from the parts: the few numbers first, then one chart, then what breaks them down, and no figure twice. `DeskCollectionResults` shows each metric and ratio in all, then the one metric the person picks, by week as one chart (columns for what is counted, a line for a share) beside the items that did best by it; the table of weeks under four small charts is gone, the chart carrying its own figures for a reader who cannot see it. `DeskFiguresView` leads with the tasks done and the runs by how they ended as figures, then what was done by week, by project and by category. `DeskItemView` shows how an item did, with the run each metric came by, before where it was used and its files, and its figures by day last. A view with nothing yet says what would fill it and draws no empty axis. The props of the three are as they were; the words `desk.records.week` and `desk.figures.runs` are gone from the language packs, and `desk.records.noFigures`, `desk.figures.lead`, `tasksDone`, `ended` and `empty` are new. `keeping-a-desk` says the order a view of figures is laid out in.
