Breaking: a pass over the pages, so that they read as one.

- A goal's page is headed by the goal, as a task's is by the task: `GoalView` takes `allHref`, the way back over the title, and puts the ways to edit the goal and to put its finished tasks away beside the title, where they stood among its facts. The part `detail` gains `sc-task-heading` and `sc-task-parent`, which were the task page's own.
- The categories are a view of the settings, their rows on shared columns, with quiet buttons. A project's settings are edited from the head of their pane, as the workspace's are.
- The feed's page is titled as its tab is named and stands on a pane. The archive keeps the board's tab lit. The page of a person's own work has its title first, and the agents that need them under it.
- A form over a list is as wide as the list, `sc-panel-form-lead`; the form that connects an agent keeps a form's width. What is said under a field keeps to the width prose is read at.
- On an agent's card the reason it stands as it does and since when are two lines, and a title is one line, cut short. On a person's card the title is not said twice. A session's badge stands beside the first line of when. Disconnecting an agent is a quiet button.
- A waiting decision asks for a word to add once an answer is chosen. A member's card says since when in the language's own order. A new project's image has the room a file's name needs, a goal's name on the months the room to be read, and a card's title fills its lines again, where it was balanced.
- The shell is drawn once the workspace's lists are known, so that the plugins' tabs do not arrive under the pointer.
