Arranging the front page, a tile one cell wide, a project's icon, carries one button for its size in place of the row of sizes, which folded over the icon on most widths and hid it. The button is the icon pack's "more", labelled with the size the tile has, and opens the list of its sizes, each by its shape and its name, over the tiles: under the button, or over it where the window has no room below for the list and more above, as for a tile at the foot of the window. Choosing one closes it, as do Escape and a press elsewhere. The list a tab group opens is placed the same way. Wider tiles keep the row, whose ends are round now, as the tile's corner is, so that it keeps clear of the corner's curve. The menu part has `sc-menu-popover`, a panel opened in the top layer over whatever around its button would clip or cover it.
