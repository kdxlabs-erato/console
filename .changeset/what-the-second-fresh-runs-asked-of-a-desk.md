What a second round of fresh agents asked of a desk's fixed files and its check.

- **A person's `AGENTS.md`** says that what changes in the desk is committed and pushed, without asking: a fresh session committed a record and asked whether to push, twice.
- **A search names a limit** wherever a desk's files and the command tell an agent to search the pack: `console skills --search <words> --limit 5`, in both `AGENTS.md` files, in the sentence `console desk` prints and in the onboarding task's desk item. One run spent 26 KB on finding a skill that was the first hit.
- **An agent account's `AGENTS.md`** says that each subagent keeps its scratch in a folder of its own under `work/`.
- **`console ask --wait <n>` as an agent account** ends a wait that ran out in the account's words, that the answer wakes it and it goes on with other work, where it still said how an attended agent keeps waiting.
- **`console desk check`** notes a use or a row of figures dated before its item was made, as `note: …`: most often a day written wrong, and no problem, since only the member knows. Notes are bounded as problems are. It notes an item that carries no status where its collection names some, too.
- **`records/README.md`** says that every item of a collection with statuses carries one, that each state an item can be in is a status, and that a rate of two metrics is a ratio, without which no rate is shown: two fresh runs left items without a status and a collection without a ratio.
