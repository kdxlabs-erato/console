A desk is given its least, and grown by what its member asks (ADR-0122).

- **The skill `keeping-a-desk`** ships with the package, beside the console's other skills: it teaches a desk's structure, its instructions and three places, each there once it is used, and how a desk is shaped to what its member asks to keep and to see: what is asked, how the shape of what is kept is chosen and written down, how a page of their own is added and what it is given, and what is checked before it is called done. Its page `collections.md` is the form for a set of things made with their uses and their figures, which a desk takes where its member asks for exactly that.
- **A desk's files are the least.** `console desk` writes `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and `.gitignore` for a person, and `console agent setup` those and `runner.json` for an agent account: no file under `records/`, and no kind of thing to keep. Both `AGENTS.md` say the structure under "This desk": the repository is the member's own, committed and pushed when something is done; what is kept lives under `records/` in the shape that fits it, pages under `web/` and projects under `work/`, each there once it is used; the file says what the desk now keeps and shows, a line each; and something new is shaped as the skill `keeping-a-desk` says. A desk written before keeps its files as they are.
- **`console desk` ends with what is next, in order,** as two numbered lines to say to an agent in that folder, or for the agent that ran it to go on with: the desk is set up as a repository, with the skill for it in the organization's pack; then the person is asked what they want to keep and to see, and the desk is shaped to it, as the skill `keeping-a-desk` says, named with its path on that machine. With `--web` it says that the pages are one empty page of the person's own until they say what they want to see. `--json` answers the two lines as `next`.
- **An agent account's desk takes the shell of its pages** from the same command: `console desk . --web`, in its desk, writes `web/` and nothing else, where it refused an agent account outright; without `--web` it still writes nothing. An agent account's `.gitignore` leaves out `web/dist/` from now on.
- **`console help desk`** names the skill `keeping-a-desk` with its place, as `console whoami` names the board's.
- **`console desk check`** reads the collections a desk keeps and leaves what else is under `records/` to the desk: a folder the index does not name is a problem only where it holds a `collection.json`, and a desk with no collection is told so, with exit 0.
