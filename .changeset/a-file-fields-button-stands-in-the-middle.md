The button of a file field stands in the middle of the field's height. A project's form draws the field for its image as tall as the fields beside it, and the button the browser gives it and the words beside the button stood against the field's top edge, with all of the room that was left under them: a browser lays the two out on a line of its own at the top of the field, whatever the field's `display` says, in Firefox, Chrome and Safari alike. The room is now the field's own padding, half above and half below.
