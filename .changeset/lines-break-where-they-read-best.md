Lines break where they read best, on every page and in a plugin's frame, which reads the same stylesheet. A sentence leaves no word alone on its last line (`text-wrap: pretty` on the page), and what is a title, a task's in a row, a decision's question, a callout's title, the words of an empty state, breaks into lines of about one length (`text-wrap: balance`), as the headings already did. A date or a time in a `time` element is never broken in two. What is typed, a field or a text area, and what is written out as it was, a `pre`, is left as it is. A browser that knows neither value wraps as before.
