# Terms

- **Task**: what is to be done, numbered per project, with its plan (a category, a goal, the days it starts and is due) and its moments (when it was started and done, which the board keeps); its owner is the one person who wrote it, and its workers are the people at work on it, any number, among whom taking a task puts the agent's person ([ADR-0037](/docs/adr/0037-a-task-has-one-owner-and-any-number-of-workers-and-a-parent-or-a-goal-is-found-by-search.md)).
- **State**: where a task stands. `idea`: nobody made it ready yet. `ready`: to be taken. `in_progress`: a run is open on it, or a person put it at work by hand. `in_review`: a run finished or failed, and a person's verdict is due. `done` and `dropped`: closed. A run moves it, by being taken, by finishing or failing, and by being abandoned ([ADR-0114](/docs/adr/0114-the-end-of-a-run-leaves-no-task-at-work-behind-it.md)); any other move is a person's word, written by hand.
- **Goal**: what the project works towards, on its roadmap, with the day it is meant to be reached by; its progress is its tasks.
- **Category**: a kind of work, the workspace's word for it, by key.
- **Run**: one agent at work on one task for one person, with its reports, what it handed in, and what it waits for. It is open while it runs or waits for a decision, and over as finished, failed or abandoned; abandoned is a run that was left, taken over by the next take, or not heard from for twelve hours.
- **Decision**: a choice that is a person's to make, kept as a record on the board: the question, the options, whom it is for ([ADR-0042](/docs/adr/0042-a-decision-is-addressed-to-a-person-its-raiser-unless-another-is-named.md)), the answer with who gave it and when, and what followed. It is the record wherever the choice was made: an answer a person gave in chat, in a meeting or by changing the task is written on it for them (working-the-board, phase 4).
- **Page**: a document of the project, Markdown at a path that does not change.
- **Project**: a board of its own, with its members and its skills address. Several repositories may share one, and a task then names the repository it is about.
- **Binding**: `.skillcdn-console.json` in a directory, written by `console use <key>`: the project, at the console's address, that the directory and those below it work in. `--project <key>` on a command, or `CONSOLE_PROJECT` in the environment, wins over it; `console whoami` says which of the three named the project, and whether the console has it.
- **Desk**: a member's own repository, a person's or an agent account's: the instructions of their agent, and their projects checked out under `work/`. A desk is bound to no project: a command of the board there says `--project <key>`, and a checkout under `work/` carries its own binding.
- **Pack**: the organization's skills and documents, read through the command: a project's own where it names an address, else the organization's, which is also what is read where no project is named.
- **SkillCDN token**: what the console reads a private pack with, `pack-token` in the API and the command: a project's own, or the workspace's for the organization's pack; connected with a code a person approves at SkillCDN, or made there by hand, by a person for the pack's repository or by the deployment's operator for a pack of its own directory, and shown to nobody.
- **Number and id**: a task has a number (`#7`), what people say and the commands take, and an id, what the API takes and `--parent` and `--goal` may; a run, a decision, a goal and a comment have ids alone. A number is its project's own: said outside the project, in another project's task or on a page, it carries the project's key (`ops#412`), as a wake does. A task whose number is not known is found by the words of its title, `console tasks --search <words>`, and a goal's id by `console goals --search <words>`. Every command that makes one prints it on a line of its own, named (`task id:`, `run id:`, `decision id:`, `goal id:`, `comment <id>:`), and `--json` prints the whole answer: an id or a number is read from there, never assumed, since several agents write on one board.
