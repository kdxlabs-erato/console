# Changing a workspace

You opened a file of a workspace. `apps/console/README.md` and `packages/console/README.md` keep the rules of their workspace under "Rules"; `deploy/README.md` is the contract for whoever operates the image, with what was learned there the hard way under "Gotchas", and it holds what there is to know of `.github/` too. Read them before you change anything in that workspace. This file holds no rule of its own; it is here so that you read those.
